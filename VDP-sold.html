Description

Just twenty two Ferrari F40's were built during the 1992 model year, benefitting from all factory updates.

We have just added to our extensive exotic car inventory a beautiful 1992 Ferrari F40 in Rosso Corsa with Rosso Vicuna cloth interior and only 3,294 miles. This F40 is loaded with: 2.9 Liter twin turbo V8, 5 speed manual transmission, center lock wheels, air conditioning and, as with all F40's, very little else. This car has extensive dealer service records with proper services dating back into the mid 90's. It was comprehensively looked over by Ferrari Maserati of Long Island in November 2020, 23 miles ago. It received a major service, a new starter (as well as a new alternator earlier in 2020), its steering rack was rebuilt, four new tires were installed (two dated 2020, two dated 2017 as they go in and out of manufacturing as needed and are equally as new), wheel alignment and horns. Previous services were done on time at the dealer in Long Island or in Fort Lauderdale. Also included are the factory manuals and keys, original tool kit and Classiche certification. This is a gorgeous, numbers matching, original chassis car in excellent condition. One of only 1,311 built overall and one of only 243 originally shipped to the USA.
